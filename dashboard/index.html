        Check a listing

        Paste a bina.az or yeniemlak.az ad link to see its price history and how it compares with similar flats.

        What this is. Asking prices from bina.az and yeniemlak.az, not sale prices. Dollar prices are converted at 1.70 ₼. A flat posted on both sites with the same rooms, area, floor and location is counted once.

        History. From the first scrape the newest ads in each section are read every 6 hours. Earlier months come from copies of bina.az ads saved by archive.org: a sample of a few hundred ads a month, not every listing, and none for some months. Those stretches are drawn dashed.

        Map. An ad is placed at its metro station, else its settlement, else its district centre. Metro stations, settlements and base map © OpenStreetMap contributors.

        “Days listed” runs from when the tracker first saw an ad until it disappeared. Districts marked * are partly inferred from the metro station or settlement in the ad.
